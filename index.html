How We Work

A proven six-phase engagement process

Every test follows a structured, industry-aligned methodology so nothing is left to chance.

1

Scoping & Authorisation

We define scope, obtain written authorisation, and set clear rules of engagement. Nothing starts without formal permission.

2

Reconnaissance

OSINT and passive information gathering to map your external footprint the way a real attacker would.

3

Scanning & Enumeration

Active discovery of hosts, services, and potential entry points using industry-standard tools.

4

Exploitation

Controlled, ethical exploitation of confirmed vulnerabilities to prove real-world impact — never destructive.

5

Post-Exploitation Analysis

We assess what an attacker could achieve after initial access and document the full attack path.

6

Reporting & Remediation

A clear, risk-rated report in plain language plus a prioritised fix-it roadmap your team can actually use.
